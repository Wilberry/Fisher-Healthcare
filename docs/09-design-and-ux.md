# Design system, navigation and user experience

## 1. Product experience

Trustworthy, accessible, mobile-first, calm and direct. Branding uses the approved Fisher Healthcare logo assets when supplied; previous design concepts and placeholder logo are not implementation dependencies. UI is developed directly in code.

## 2. Patient public navigation

Home; Tests & Pricing; How It Works; Service Areas; About & Trust; Help/Contact; Sign In; Book a Collection. The mobile header must prioritize test discovery and booking without crowding.

## 3. Authenticated patient navigation

Dashboard; Bookings; Results; Family Members; Addresses; Payments & Receipts; Support; Account & Privacy. Show upcoming visit, recent status and available results without exposing unauthorized relatives' information.

## 4. Operations navigation

Overview; Orders; Dispatch; Specimens; Laboratories; Exceptions; Customers/Support; Finance; Reports; Staff & Permissions; Settings/Audit. Display module items according to role.

## 5. Field navigation

Today's Tasks; Assignment Detail; Collection Checklist; Specimens; Handoffs; Incidents; Sync Status; Profile. Large tap targets, legible typography, explicit confirmations and safe offline indicators.

## 6. Booking UX

Step 1 tests/lab; step 2 tier and actual available slot; step 3 patient and location; step 4 review and payment; step 5 confirmation and tracking. Allow quote exploration without mandatory signup. Present exact prices, preparation instructions and collection windows before payment. Explain conditional turnaround.

## 7. Component standards

Shared design tokens for colors, typography, spacing, radii, focus and status. Support responsive layouts, keyboard interaction, screen reader labels, accessible form errors, loading/empty/error states, consistent date/time/currency formatting and WCAG 2.2 AA target. Avoid color-only status communication.

## 8. Results UX

Released results appear in the patient dashboard with test name, lab, sample date, release date and secure view/download action. Email delivery is a parallel approved channel; failed email does not remove dashboard access. Caregiver result views require explicit scope.

## 9. Visual acceptance

Review mobile and desktop screens, real-world low-bandwidth behavior, elderly-friendly readability, form recovery, validation, accessible contrast and realistic clinical copy. Never use fictitious testimonials, lab credentials or availability as production claims.
