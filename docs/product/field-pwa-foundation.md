# Fisher Healthcare Field Application — Foundation

**Programme phase:** Phase 1 — Product foundation and information architecture

The field application will support authorized collection personnel and couriers through a mobile-first installation experience. Its application manifest establishes the identity and presentation of the installable interface; it does not enable offline access to assignments or clinical records.

Operational task assignment, patient verification, custody capture, secure offline persistence, deduplicated synchronization, and supervised field acceptance belong to Phase 3. Until those controls have passed verification, the field interface remains a foundation preview with no patient data.

The future offline data model will require device-bound authentication, controlled data retention, encrypted local storage where supported, server-issued task scopes, conflict resolution, and explicit delivery acknowledgments. Clinical records must not be cached indiscriminately by a service worker.
